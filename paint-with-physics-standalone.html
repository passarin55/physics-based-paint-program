How Paint With Physics works

Paint with materials that obey physics. While physics is paused the canvas is an ordinary painting; run it and everything you painted starts to behave.

Canvas

  • Water flows downhill and levels out, oil (0.80 g/cm³) floats on it, sand piles up at its angle of repose, snow melts, and lava boils water and cools to basalt.
  • Fire rises on Navier–Stokes buoyancy, ignites wood, oil, plants and gunpowder, and leaves smoke and ash. Hydrogen detonates into water vapour.
  • Electricity: draw copper from a power cell. Pulses travel through conductors, heat steel filaments until they glow, bubble hydrogen out of brine and arc across gaps.
  • Magnets: paint N and S poles; iron filings and ferrofluid follow −∇|H|². Above 770 °C, the Curie point, a magnet becomes plain steel.
  • Analysis views show temperature, air velocity, charge, magnetic field and density.

Formulas

  • Fifteen formulas: Navier–Stokes, the Riemann zeta function, the BBP π digit formula, gamma and digamma, trigonometry, calculus, Fourier series, the central limit theorem, Newtonian gravity, Mandelbrot and Julia sets, the Lorenz attractor, the golden angle, L-systems, Planck’s law and your own f(x, y).
  • In this workspace the canvas brush paints with the selected formula: its colours, its region, its graph along your stroke, its curve as a repeating figure, its force field, or its computed digits. Stamps, fills and full-canvas force fields are still one click away.

Chemistry

  • Molecules are particles. Pick an experiment, inject reagents, change temperature, pressure, solvent, catalyst and indicator, or grab one molecule and fling it at another. Watch pH, heat, composition and each elementary step of the mechanism.

Spectrum

  • A virtual IT-200U, 723PC or 721 reads the bench through Beer–Lambert, with a real slit width and stray light. Import a real spectrum, fit kinetics, calibrate, and ask Claude to interpret it.

Kamvas pen

  • Pressure sets the brush size, tilt sprays, the eraser end erases and the barrel button picks a material. Palm rejection ignores touches while the pen is near.
